OVERVIEW

Experience the magic of Dubai where futuristic skyscrapers meet rich Arabian heritage. Shop in lavish malls, experience the thrill of a desert safari, take a luxurious yacht cruise, and witness stunning architecture that defies imagination. From the world's tallest building to pristine beaches, Dubai offers an unforgettable blend of adventure, luxury, and culture.

  • Round-trip airfare
  • 5-star hotel accommodation
  • Daily breakfast
  • Desert safari with BBQ dinner
  • Burj Khalifa tickets (124th floor)
  • Dubai Marina yacht cruise
  • Airport transfers
  • Professional tour guide

What's Not Included

  • Lunch and dinner (except desert BBQ)
  • Travel insurance
  • Visa fees

  • Tips and gratuities
