Requirements & Eligibility

Application Requirements

To ensure a smooth and legal recruitment process for working in America, applicants must meet specific documentation and fitness requirements. Our team reviews every application carefully to match you with the right employer and role.

Our coordinators will assist you with verification, visa processing, and travel arrangements once eligibility is confirmed.
  • Valid passport (scanned copy)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Age between 21 and 45 years
  • Physically fit and willing to work
  • Previous experience (optional, depending on position)
  • Willingness to relocate and adapt to American working conditions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the American visa process usually take? ▼
American visa processing typically takes 3-6 weeks depending on the visa type and government processing times. We keep you informed at every stage and ensure all documentation is prepared correctly to avoid delays.
What kind of accommodation will I receive in America? ▼
Most employers provide shared accommodation with basic amenities including beds, kitchen facilities, and utilities. The exact type depends on your employer and job role. We assist in ensuring your accommodation meets acceptable American standards.
Can I choose which American state to work in? ▼
We take your preference into consideration, but final placement depends on job availability, employer requirements, and your qualifications. We discuss all options with you before confirming any placement.
Will I need to learn English before coming to America? ▼
Basic English proficiency is helpful but not always required depending on the position. We provide language support resources and can connect you with English classes if needed. Many employers offer language training as part of their onboarding process.
How will I know my job role and location in America? ▼
Once your application is reviewed and an employer is identified, you'll receive a formal job offer letter detailing your role, salary, location, accommodation, and all employment terms. Nothing is finalized without your full knowledge and consent.
