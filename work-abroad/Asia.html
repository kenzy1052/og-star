Requirements & Eligibility

Application Requirements

To ensure a smooth and legal recruitment process for working in Asia, applicants must meet specific documentation and fitness requirements. Our team reviews every application carefully to match you with the right employer and role.

Our coordinators will assist you with verification, visa processing, and travel arrangements once eligibility is confirmed.
  • Valid passport (scanned copy)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Age between 21 and 45 years
  • Physically fit and willing to work
  • Previous experience (optional, depending on position)
  • Willingness to relocate and adapt to Asian working conditions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the Asian visa process usually take? ▼
Asian visa processing typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on the country and visa type. Some countries like Singapore and Japan may take longer due to stricter requirements. We keep you informed at every stage and ensure all documentation is prepared correctly.
Do I need to speak an Asian language to work there? ▼
Not necessarily. English is widely spoken in many Asian countries, especially in business and education sectors. However, learning basic local phrases can be helpful for daily life. We provide language resources and can connect you with language classes if needed.
Can I choose which Asian country to work in? ▼
We take your preference into consideration, but final placement depends on job availability, employer requirements, and your qualifications. We discuss all options with you before confirming any placement.
What about cultural differences in Asian workplaces? ▼
Asian workplaces often have different cultural norms and work ethics. We provide cultural orientation sessions to help you adapt. Many employers also offer cultural training as part of their onboarding process to ensure smooth integration.
How will I know my job role and location in Asia? ▼
Once your application is reviewed and an employer is identified, you'll receive a formal job offer letter detailing your role, salary, location, accommodation, and all employment terms. Nothing is finalized without your full knowledge and consent.
