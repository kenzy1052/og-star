Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What documents do I need for UAE visa processing? ▼
For UAE visa processing, you'll need a valid passport with at least 6 months validity, recent passport-sized photographs, and a completed visa application form. Additional documents may be required depending on your specific visa type.
How long does the visa processing take? ▼
Visa processing times vary depending on the type of visa and your country of origin. Generally, it takes between 3-7 working days for standard visas. Express services are available for urgent travel needs.
Do you provide accommodation assistance? ▼
Yes, we provide comprehensive accommodation assistance. We can help you find suitable housing based on your preferences and budget, whether you're looking for short-term stays or long-term arrangements.
Can you help with job placement in the UAE? ▼
Absolutely! We have partnerships with reputable employers across various sectors in the UAE. We can help match your skills and qualifications with suitable job opportunities and guide you through the entire recruitment process.
