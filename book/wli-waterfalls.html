Wli Waterfalls

Volta Region, Ghana

Overview

Wli Waterfalls, also known as Agumatsa Falls, is the highest waterfall in Ghana and West Africa, cascading from a height of approximately 143 meters (469 feet). Located in the Wli Nature Reserve near the village of Wli in the Volta Region, this magnificent natural wonder is surrounded by lush tropical forest and offers a breathtaking spectacle of nature's power and beauty.

The waterfall consists of two main sections: the upper falls and the lower falls. The lower falls are easily accessible and popular among tourists, while the upper falls require a more challenging hike through the forest. The area is home to a diverse ecosystem with hundreds of butterfly species, birds, and the famous Mona monkeys that inhabit the surrounding forest. The local community considers the falls sacred and believes they have spiritual significance.

Travel Information

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is during the dry season from November to March when the trails are less slippery and the water flow is still impressive. The rainy season (April to October) offers the most powerful water flow but can make hiking challenging.

Difficulty Level

The lower falls are easily accessible with a 45-minute walk on a relatively flat trail. The upper falls require a more challenging 2-3 hour hike through the forest with some steep sections. Good hiking shoes are recommended.

Duration

Plan for a half-day trip to visit the lower falls (3-4 hours total). For both upper and lower falls, allow a full day (6-7 hours) including hiking, swimming, and relaxation time at the falls.

Fun Facts & Nearby Attractions

Wli Waterfalls is the highest waterfall in Ghana and one of the tallest in West Africa, with water cascading from a height of approximately 143 meters.

The surrounding forest is home to over 200 species of birds and 400 species of butterflies, as well as Mona monkeys that can often be seen playing in the trees.

Visitors can swim in the natural pool at the base of the lower falls, which is refreshingly cool and provides a perfect way to relax after the hike.

The nearby Afadjato Mountain (Ghana's highest peak) and Tagbo Falls can be visited in the same trip for a complete nature experience in the Volta Region.
