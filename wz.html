Requirements & Eligibility

What you need to get started

Application Requirements

To ensure a smooth and legal recruitment process, applicants must meet basic documentation and fitness requirements. Our team reviews every application carefully to match you with the right employer and role.

Our coordinators will assist you with verification, visa processing, and travel arrangements once eligibility is confirmed.
  • Valid passport (scanned copy)
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • Age between 21 and 45 years
  • Physically fit and willing to work
  • Previous experience (optional, depending on position)
  • Willingness to relocate and adapt to UAE working conditions

Choosing Your Destination

Dubai or Abu Dhabi

Dubai

Known for its modern skyline, rapid growth, and vibrant city life, Dubai offers countless opportunities for workers across construction, hospitality, and security sectors. It's a fast-paced environment where ambition meets opportunity.

Abu Dhabi

As the capital of the UAE, Abu Dhabi provides a more structured and calm environment with long-term job stability and excellent living conditions. It's ideal for workers seeking growth in a professional and balanced setting.

You may be placed in either Dubai or Abu Dhabi depending on the job role, employer, and your personal preference, all handled with transparency and care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered

How long does visa processing usually take? ▼
Visa processing typically takes 2-4 weeks depending on the employer and government processing times. We keep you informed at every stage and ensure all documentation is prepared correctly to avoid delays.
What kind of accommodation will I receive? ▼
Most employers provide shared accommodation with basic amenities including beds, kitchen facilities, and utilities. The exact type depends on your employer and job role. We assist in ensuring your accommodation meets acceptable standards.
Can I choose between Dubai and Abu Dhabi? ▼
We take your preference into consideration, but final placement depends on job availability, employer requirements, and your qualifications. We discuss all options with you before confirming any placement.
Will I pay any fees before traveling? ▼
All fee structures are explained transparently from the beginning. Some documentation and processing costs may apply, but we provide a clear breakdown. No hidden fees are involved, and all costs are discussed upfront.
How will I know my job role and location? ▼
Once your application is reviewed and an employer is identified, you'll receive a formal job offer letter detailing your role, salary, location, accommodation, and all employment terms. Nothing is finalized without your full knowledge and consent.
